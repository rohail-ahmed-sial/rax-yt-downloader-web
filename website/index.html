Questions

Where do the files go?

Into your browser's normal Downloads folder, on whichever device you used. On a phone, look in the Files or Downloads app.

Which quality should I pick?

1080p looks sharp on laptops and phones. 720p saves space. 4K files are large and take longer, and only some videos have them. For music, MP3 at 192 kbps is plenty.

Can I download a whole playlist?

Yes. Paste the playlist link, pick the quality once, untick anything you don't want and press Download. Your browser may ask whether this site can download several files. Choose Allow.

Why won't a video download?

Private and members-only videos can't be saved. Age-restricted videos need a cookies.txt file, which HOW TO USE.txt explains. If lots of videos suddenly fail, close the black window and run START.bat again. It updates the download engine every time, and that fixes most problems after YouTube changes something.

Does the site work without the black window?

No. That window is the downloader itself. Keep it open while you use the site and close it when you're done.

Can I put this site on my web hosting?

Not on normal PHP hosting. The site needs Python and FFmpeg running next to it, so it's built to run on your own computer and serve the phones on your Wi-Fi.
